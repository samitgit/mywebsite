About Me

I am a doctoral student in the Department of Mathematics & Statistics at Old Dominion University (ODU), concentrating in Mathematics in Data Science. My academic journey began at the University of Dhaka, Bangladesh, where I earned my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics. I later completed my M.S. in Data Science at ODU, which strengthened my foundation in statistical analysis, computational methods, and applied machine learning.

My research focuses on machine learning, statistical modeling, and the optimization of ML algorithms. I am particularly interested in developing efficient, interpretable, and scalable computational approaches that bridge theoretical mathematics with real-world data-driven applications.

I am passionate about leveraging mathematical insights to improve algorithmic performance and contribute to advancing data science methodologies. I enjoy collaborative research, interdisciplinary problem-solving, and exploring innovative techniques at the intersection of mathematics and modern data analytics.

Let's explore the data and innovations in these fields. If something is on your mind, feel free to connect with me to discuss research, collaboration opportunities, or anything related to data science and applied mathematics.
